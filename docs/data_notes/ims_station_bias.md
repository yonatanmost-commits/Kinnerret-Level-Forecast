## Findings

- **Temperature and wind offsets are geographic and stable for 20+ years.** Lev Kinneret (mid-lake) has warmer nights
  (daily min about +4.2 °C), cooler afternoons (daily max about −1.6 °C) and stronger wind (about +1.1 m/s, ~45% higher) than
  Tzemach (south shore). The offsets match before and after 2012.
- **Humidity has a step between late 2012 and spring 2013.** Lev − Tzemach goes from about −3 to −7 points (2004–2012)
  to about +2 to +5 (2013 onwards). Against Beit Tzaida (from 2012), Tzemach drops about 4–5 points around 2013-01, and Lev rises
  about 4–5 points around 2013-04. Which sensor was right can't be told from the data; IMS sensor-replacement records
  would settle it. Humidity before 2013 is therefore not comparable with humidity after, at either station, and VPD and ET0 inherit this.
- **Rain:** Lev Kinneret's gauge is effectively empty before 2012 (and dead in 2026). On the 2,205 shared days since 2012,
  Lev Kinneret totals 0.76× Tzemach, and the two agree on wet vs dry 96% of the time.

## Coverage of the pre-2012 download (IMS envista feed, 10-min)

Tzemach: temperature, humidity, wind and radiation from 1994-01; rain from 1998-02; 91–98% of 10-min slots; no dropped months left.
Lev Kinneret: from 2003-11; about 90%; 3 months per channel never returned (spring 2012 outage).
Full month-by-month table: docs/experiments/ims_pre2012_coverage.csv. Raw cache: Raw Data/IMS envista pre-2012/.

## Decision (user, 2026-10-16)

Option A: gold uses Tzemach as the primary station for every year, so the record has no station switch. Built on a branch, the
champion is retrained and compared before anything reaches master.

## Real gaps in the IMS archive (months returned as all `-`, confirmed by re-fetch)

These are not dropped requests. The feed returns a full month of records with every value `-`, and fetching again gives the same.
Re-fetch check 2026-10-16: Tzemach humidity 1995-06 (4,308 records, 0 numeric) and 2003-01 (4,463, 0 numeric).

Within the gold span (from 1998-02), only two Tzemach gaps fall inside it: **humidity 2003-01** and **radiation 2006-09**.
With Tzemach as the primary station, those months fall back to Lev Kinneret for humidity (absent in 2003-01, so that month stays
empty) and have no fallback for radiation (Tzemach is the only radiation sensor). So VPD/ET0 are missing for those two months.
